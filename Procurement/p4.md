P4 testing

Test it in this order.

1. Full receipt

Create a PO for 100 units.

Receive 100.

Expected:

PO → Fully Received

and the receipt shows:

Ordered: 100
Received: 100
Accepted: 100
2. Partial receipt

PO = 100.

First GR = 40.

Second GR = 30.

Third GR = 30.

Expected:

40 + 30 + 30 = 100

and the PO only becomes Fully Received after the third receipt.

3. Over-receiving

PO = 100.

Attempt to receive 101.

Expected:

BLOCKED

Then configure a 5% tolerance and test 105.

4. Rejection

Receive:

100 units
90 accepted
10 rejected

Verify the rejection reason is mandatory and the accepted quantity is only 90.

5. Damage

Receive 100.

Record 5 as damaged.

Verify damaged quantity is separately tracked and isn't silently treated as accepted inventory.

6. Service receipt

Create a PO for a consulting service.

Create a Service Receipt.

Verify no normal stock movement is generated.

7. Batch item

For a batch-controlled item, attempt receipt without a batch number.

Must be blocked.

Then provide:

BATCH-2026-001

and expiry date.

It should succeed.

8. Serial item

For a serial-controlled item, enter the same serial number twice.

Must be rejected.

9. Inventory integration

For a stock item:

PO → GR → Accepted Qty → Inventory Movement

Verify stock is updated through the inventory engine, not by a direct quantity update from the GR page.

10. Transaction rollback

Force an inventory-posting failure during GR finalization.

Verify:

No partially finalized receipt exists.

This is one of the most important tests in P4.

11. Cancellation

Finalize a receipt, then cancel it.

If inventory was posted, verify the system uses a reversal, rather than deleting the original receipt/movement.

12. Traceability

You should now be able to navigate:

PR → RFQ → Quotation → Award → PO → GR

and from the PO see its complete receiving history.

13. Accounting boundary

Create and finalize a normal GR.

Verify that a vendor bill/AP liability has NOT been accidentally created merely because the goods were received.

Once P4 passes, the next phase is P5 — Vendor Bills / Accounts Payable Integration, where we'll connect PO + accepted receipts + vendor invoice → 2-way/3-way matching → AP liability → Finance posting.
